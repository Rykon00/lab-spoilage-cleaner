Version: 1.0.0
Date: 25. 10. 2026
  Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Empties the lab trash (where science packs that spoil inside a lab leave their spoilage) and takes items the lab cannot use out of its input slots.
    - Mode "Robots pick it up" (default): the items are dropped next to the lab and marked for deconstruction, construction robots carry them to a storage chest. Only for labs inside a construction area, nothing is dropped elsewhere.
    - Mode "Directly into the logistic network": the items go straight into the logistic network the lab stands in.
    - Labs on space platforms put their spoilage into the platform hub (setting, default on).
    - Labs are checked in turns, spread over the check interval (setting, default 2 seconds).
    - Console command /lsc-clean cleans every lab right now.
    - English and German locale.
  Info:
    - Added a thumbnail (vanilla icons): lab, spoilage and a construction robot carrying it away.
